## Use Cases

This page helps you:

- **Monitor withdrawal pressure**: Track the queued and withdrawable layers relative to active eUSX to gauge how much capital is exiting the YieldVault
- **Assess queue duration preference**: Compare 1-day vs 7-day queued volumes to understand depositor urgency when unlocking
- **Evaluate USX stablecoin adoption**: Toggle to USX Supply to see total supply growth and what share is locked in the YieldVault
- **Identify large unlock events**: The queued positions table surfaces the biggest individual unlocks still pending maturity

## Methodology

<details>
<summary>Click to expand technical details</summary>

**Key Concepts**

- **eUSX**: A yield-bearing token representing locked USX in the Solstice YieldVault. Its value grows as yield accrues
- **Unlock**: The event of initiating a withdrawal. Unlocking burns eUSX and puts the underlying USX into a queue with a maturity period (1 day or 7 days)
- **Queued**: The state of USX after an unlock event, waiting for the maturity period to pass before it can be withdrawn
- **USX**: The Solstice stablecoin, minted by depositing USDC collateral

**Queued Balance Calculation**

- **Queued 1d**: USX unlocked with 1-day duration on the current day (matures the next day, so only today's unlocks are queued at any point)
- **Queued 7d**: Sum of USX unlocked with 7-day duration over the past 7 days (each matures 7 days after its unlock date)
- **Withdrawable**: Matured positions not yet withdrawn = cumulative unlocks - cumulative withdrawals - currently queued
- Duration is determined on-chain from the `unlock_ready_at` timestamp in the eUSX unlock event

**eUSX Withdrawal Queue**

- **Formula**: (queued + withdrawable) / (vault USX balance + queued + withdrawable)
- Combines both pending (not yet matured) and withdrawable (matured but not claimed) USX
- All terms are in USX, giving the fraction of total eUSX-related USX in the withdrawal pipeline

**USX Locked in YieldVault**

- **Formula**: vault USX balance / USX supply * 100
- Shows what percentage of the total USX stablecoin supply is locked in the YieldVault

**Queued Positions**

- Individual unlock events are extracted from on-chain transaction data
- A position is "queued" if its maturity timestamp has not yet passed
- Table shows the 10 largest by USX value

**Data Sources**

- eUSX supply and USX supply are tracked via pre/post token balance deltas across all on-chain transactions
- Unlock durations are extracted from the unlock event payload (86,400s = 1 day, 604,800s = 7 days)

**Data Update Frequency**

Data is automatically updated once per day. Check the "Last updated" timestamp in the site header for data freshness.

**Known Limitations**

- **Queue approximation**: The sliding-window model assumes all unlocks mature exactly at their duration boundary. Intra-day timing differences may cause minor discrepancies
- **Position tracking**: Only recent days (last 7-8 days) are scanned for queued positions. Older daily records without per-event data will show no positions

</details>
