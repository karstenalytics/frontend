# Active Stakers

Tracks the number of unique wallets holding active TUNA stakes over time. The count increases when new wallets stake and decreases when wallets fully unstake.

## Current Metrics

## Use Cases

This chart helps you:

- **Track protocol adoption**: Monitor growth in the number of wallets participating in TUNA staking
- **Assess user retention**: Identify periods of staker growth vs. churn (unstaking)
- **Evaluate engagement trends**: Compare active staker counts with [wallet usage](/analysis/defituna/adoption/wallet-usage) for daily and weekly active wallets
- **Assess whale concentration**: Use the Top 50 Stakers table to see how staking power is distributed among the largest holders
- **Correlate with revenue**: Cross-reference staker growth with [revenue trends](/analysis/defituna/fees-revenue/by-type) to understand protocol health

**Active Stakers Time Series:**
- **X-axis**: Date (UTC days)
- **Left Y-axis (area line)**: Number of unique wallets with staked TUNA balance > 0.001 (dust threshold)
- **Right Y-axis (bars)**: Daily gross movement - green bars show wallets that became active ("New Stakers"), red bars show wallets that dropped below the dust threshold ("Fully Unstaked")
- **New Stakers**: Wallets whose staked balance crossed above the dust threshold on that day. The vast majority are first-time stakers, but the count also includes a small number of wallets that previously fully unstaked and later re-staked.
- **Fully Unstaked**: Wallets whose staked balance fell to or below the dust threshold (0.001 TUNA), including full unstakes

**Top 50 Stakers Table:**
- **Rank**: Position by current staked balance (descending)
- **Address**: Solana address of the staker (links to Solscan)
- **Current Stake**: Current staked TUNA balance
- **% of Total**: Wallet's share of all currently staked TUNA
- **vs. Peak**: Current balance as a percentage of the wallet's all-time-high staked balance (100% = at peak, lower = has partially unstaked)
- **30d Balance %**: Percent change in the wallet's own staked TUNA balance vs. the 30-day snapshot. "--" if wallet was first seen after the 30-day snapshot date
- **30d DEX Net**: Net USD value of market buys minus sells over the last 30 days, sourced from [Nansen](https://nansen.ai). Shows whether the wallet has been a net buyer (+) or seller (-) on DEXes. "--" if the wallet does not appear in Nansen's trader data.
- **Timeline**: "See details" button linking to the wallet's full staking timeline page

## Methodology

<details>
<summary>Click to expand technical details</summary>

**Data Sources**

- **Staking transactions**: On-chain staking instructions from DefiTuna staking program
- **Staker cache**: Compressed event log containing all staking activity
- **[Nansen](https://nansen.ai)**: 30-day DEX trader data (buy/sell volumes) for the TUNA token

**Active Staker Calculation**

A wallet is counted as an **active staker** on a given day if:
- Its staked TUNA balance > 0.001 at 23:59:59 UTC
- The balance is calculated by processing all stake/unstake events chronologically

**Summary Cards**

- **Active Stakers**: Number of wallets with staked TUNA > 0.001 at the latest data point. Change badge shows the percentage change vs. 30 days ago.
- **Top 50 Concentration**: Sum of the "% of Total" column for the 50 largest stakers. Change badge shows the percentage point difference vs. the top-50 concentration 30 days ago (recomputed from the snapshot's top 50).
- **Top 50 at Peak**: Percentage of the current top 50 stakers whose staked balance equals their all-time high. First-time stakers are trivially at their peak and are included. Change badge shows the percentage point difference vs. the snapshot 30 days ago.

**30-Day Change Calculation**

All 30-day change badges compare the current value against a snapshot taken at the most recent day with data at or before 30 days ago. On sparse datasets (e.g. a quiet weekend with no events), the snapshot may be slightly older than 30 days.

**Top Stakers Ranking**

The Top 50 Stakers table ranks by:
- **Current staked balance**: Wallets sorted by their TUNA balance at the latest data point (descending)
- **% of Total**: Each wallet's share of the total staked TUNA across all wallets
- **vs. Peak**: Compares current balance to the wallet's all-time-high staked balance, showing retention
- **30d Balance %**: Percent change in each wallet's own staked TUNA balance between now and the 30-day snapshot
- **30d DEX Net**: Net of DEX buys minus sells (USD) over 30 days from Nansen. Independent of staking activity — a wallet can be a net buyer on DEXes while unstaking, or vice versa

**Active Staker Threshold**

A wallet is counted as active when its replayed staked balance exceeds 0.001 tokens. Summing thousands of floating-point stake/unstake deltas in the browser can produce residual rounding dust (e.g. 10^-10) for wallets whose true balance is zero. The threshold eliminates these false positives and ensures the count matches the server-side computation on the Overview page, which uses fixed-precision decimal arithmetic.

**Update Frequency**

Data is automatically updated once a day. Check the "Last updated" timestamp in the site header for data freshness.
